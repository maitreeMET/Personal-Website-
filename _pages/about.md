I am an undergraduate in UC Berkeley's **M.E.T. dual-degree program**, studying Electrical Engineering and Computer Science and Business Administration at Haas.
I do machine learning research at **Berkeley AI Research (BAIR)** in the EMBER Centre, advised by [Jonas Frey](https://jonasfrey96.github.io/) and Siming He in Prof. Jitendra Malik's and Prof. Claire Tomlin's labs.

I study how robots should decide where to look when time is limited, and what kind of expert demonstrations best teach that behavior to vision-language models.
My first-author paper, [Racing the Clock](/assets/pdf/racing-the-clock.pdf), won the **Best Paper Award** at the IROS 2026 Workshop on Intelligent Information Gathering.
